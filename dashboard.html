Withdraw to Phantom

Stop the live bot with Control-C and manage open positions first. Preview the withdrawal in Terminal; no transfer is made by this page.

printf 'Paste Phantom SOL receiving address: ' && read -r phantom_address && .venv/bin/python wallet_manage.py withdraw --to "$phantom_address" --sol 0.01

Replace the destination and amount. To submit the reviewed plan, repeat with --execute, then type WITHDRAW at the local prompt. --all replaces --sol to withdraw remaining SOL minus the quoted fee. Never share the wallet file.
